3x3 Macropad Case Specifications
=================================

Print Settings:
- Material: PLA or PETG
- Layer Height: 0.2mm (0.1mm for details)
- Infill: 20%
- Support: Yes (for overhangs >45°)
- Wall Thickness: 1.5mm
- No raft needed

Dimensions (approx):
- Length: ~70mm
- Width: ~70mm
- Height: 13mm (3mm base + 10mm walls)

Mounting:
- Heatset inserts: M3 (4.7mm holes, 4mm deep)
- Screw type: M3x4mm
- Count: 4 mounting points

Post-Processing:
- Remove supports carefully
- Sand edges with 180–220 grit
- Optional: Paint or coat with clear gloss finish
