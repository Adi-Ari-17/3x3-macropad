# Build Guide – 3×3 Macropad

## Prerequisites
- Soldering iron & solder (60/40 or lead-free)
- Tweezers & small screwdriver
- Flux (optional but recommended)

## Step-by-Step Assembly

### 1. Prepare the PCB
- Inspect for defects
- Apply flux to pads if needed

### 2. Solder Diodes
- Orientation: Cathode (black line) toward column pin
- Solder one pad, then adjust, then solder second pad

### 3. Solder XIAO Microcontroller
- Align pins carefully
- Solder one corner pin first
- Solder remaining pins

### 4. Solder USB Connector
- Position with pads aligned
- Solder all 5 pins

### 5. Solder Hotswap Sockets
- Insert each socket into pads
- Solder both pads per socket (repeat for all 9)

### 6. Test with Multimeter
- Check for continuity between GND and key pads
- Verify no shorts

### 7. Prepare Case
- Remove supports from 3D-printed parts
- Sand edges smooth
- Insert heatset inserts (if using)

### 8. Assemble Case
- Place PCB in bottom enclosure
- Screw top plate to mounting holes
- Secure side walls

### 9. Install Switches
- Insert 9 switches into hotswap sockets
- Press down firmly until click

### 10. Keycaps
- Add your favorite keycaps
- Enjoy!

## Troubleshooting
- Keys not registering? Check diode orientation & solder joints
- USB not detected? Verify XIAO pins & USB connector solder
